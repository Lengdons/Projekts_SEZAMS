Electronic Ascending-Bid Auction of Newly Formed Real Estate at Eksporta Street 3

The Liepāja Special Economic Zone Authority, unified registration No. LV90000329402, is holding an electronic ascending-bid auction for newly formed real estate at Eksporta Street 3, Liepāja, comprising one land plot and 12 buildings, with a total land area of 51,521 m².

Starting price: EUR 680,000.00 (excluding any applicable VAT); bid increment: EUR 6,800.00; auction security deposit: EUR 68,000.00.

The purchase price shall be paid as follows:

  • Within 10 working days after the preliminary agreement enters into force, the purchaser shall pay an advance of 30% of the purchase price.
  • The remainder shall be paid within two weeks after the purchase agreement is signed.

The auction starts on 6 October 2026 at 13:00 and ends on 5 November 2026 at 13:00.

Participants may register from 6 October 2026 at 13:00 until 26 October 2026 at 23:59 at the electronic auction website https://izsoles.ta.gov.lv.

To participate, a person must pay the security deposit of 10% of the starting price, i.e. EUR 68,000.00, before the registration deadline into the account of the Liepāja Special Economic Zone Authority, registration No. LV90000329402, AS “SEB banka”, account No. LV42UNLA0012000609480, with the payment reference “Security deposit for the auction of real estate at Eksporta Street 3, Liepāja”.

The auction is held electronically at https://izsoles.ta.gov.lv. Enquiries: telephone 29294267, email raivo.spaks@lsez.lv.
