Repeated Ascending-Bid Auction for Property on Naglu Street

Regarding the results of the real estate auction:

On 27 July 2026, the electronic auction of the property at Naglu iela 1, Liepāja, organised by the Liepāja Special Economic Zone Authority, concluded. The highest bid of EUR 297,950.00 (two hundred and ninety-seven thousand nine hundred and fifty euros), excluding value added tax, was made by FLESS Liepāja Special Economic Zone SIA, registration No. 40203373393, registered address: Satiksmes iela 6, Liepāja (the auction results were approved at the LSEZ Board meeting on 19 August 2026, Board Decision No. 66).

______________________________

 

The Liepāja Special Economic Zone Authority, unified registration No. LV90000329402, is holding a repeated electronic ascending-bid auction for the property at Naglu iela 1, Liepāja, cadastral No. 17000220152, comprising a building with cadastral designation 17000220059027 and a land plot with a total area of 15,048 m2.

The starting price is EUR 295,000.00 (two hundred and ninety-five thousand euros and 00 cents), the bid increment is EUR 2,950.00 (two thousand nine hundred and fifty euros and 00 cents), and the auction security deposit is EUR 29,500.00 (twenty-nine thousand five hundred euros and 00 cents). Payment of the purchase price shall be made as follows:

  • Within 10 working days after the preliminary agreement enters into force, the purchaser shall make an advance payment of 30% (thirty percent) of the purchase price.
  • The purchaser shall pay the remaining purchase price within two weeks after signing the purchase agreement.

The auction begins on 19 June 2026 at 13:00 and ends on 20 July 2026 at 13:00.

Bidders may register for the auction from 19 June 2026 at 13:00 until 9 July 2026 at 23:59 on the electronic auction website: https://izsoles.ta.gov.lv.

Anyone wishing to participate in the auction must pay a security deposit of 10% (ten percent) of the starting price of the auctioned property, i.e. EUR 29,500.00 (twenty-nine thousand five hundred euros and 00 cents), before the registration deadline. The deposit must be transferred to the LSEZ Authority's bank account: Liepāja Special Economic Zone Authority, unified registration No. LV90000329402, AS "SEB banka", account No. LV42UNLA0012000609480, with the payment reference "Auction security deposit for the property at Naglu iela 1".

The auction takes place electronically at: https://izsoles.ta.gov.lv. For information about the repeated real estate auction, call 29294267 or email raivo.spaks@lsez.lv.
