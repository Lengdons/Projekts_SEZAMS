  • Ferry Traffic
The ferry traffic from Liepaja to Travemunde (Germany) 6 times a week is provided in cooperation with the Swedish owned RO-PAX operator "Stena Line", which has established a vast ferry traffic network to a number of destinations in Europe.

www.stenalinefreight.com

www.stenaline.lv


At the moment, Stena Line Ports Liepājas SEZ Ltd. provides continuous operation of ferry lines servicing RO-PAX type of ferries on the following route Liepaja - Travemunde, Germany (6x a week).

The company is operating a specialized ferry terminal, which is capable of attending up to 20 ferries a week.

Stena Line

“Stena Line” one of the world’s largest ferry operators with over 26,000 yearly sailings.
