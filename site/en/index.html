Invest in Liepaja

The Liepaja SEZ Authority and the municipality are determined on establishing an attractive business environment - a unified economic ecosystem with the aim of promoting development of the city and the region, attracting new entrepreneurs, and supporting expansion of existing companies, creating new jobs with competitive salaries, increasing GDP and the amount of taxes paid.

Find out more

The Port

The Port of Liepaja is the third largest port in Latvia and the city's gateway to the wider world. The Port of Liepaja brings the essence and character of the city. Liepaja, like all cities that have their own ports, think and live more broadly. The Port of Liepaja has a favorable geographical location between east and west.

Find out more

Development projects

In synergy with business companies, the Liepaja SEZ Authority plans long-term development and develops projects to promote establishment of new companies based on the basic principles of circular economy and technological growth.

Find out more

Liepaja Marina

The Liepaja Marina is the only one in Latvia that boasts compliance with 37 requirements of the international eco-certificate Blue Flag. The marina is located in its historical place - in the city center, which allows tourists to easily enjoy the city's attractions. The city center, the Rose Square and the Tourist Information Center are within a five-minute walking distance, and the beach itself is within a fifteen minute-walk.

Find out more

The Airport

The Liepaja Airport is one of the two international airports in Latvia, which after reconstruction was the first airport in Europe to undergo the certification process according to the recently approved EU regulations.

Find out more

Oskars Kalpaks Bridge

The swing bridge is one of the oldest metal structure bridges in Latvia, opened in 1906. The Karosta Canal is a busy shipping route, so the bridge is opened for ship traffic several times a day. In order to find out whether Oskars Kalpaks Bridge is open, a webcam has been installed.
