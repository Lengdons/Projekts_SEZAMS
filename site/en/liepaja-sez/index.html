LSEZ

Under the Law on the Liepāja Special Economic Zone, adopted on 17 February 1997, the Liepāja Special Economic Zone comprises the territory of the Liepāja Special Economic Zone, where companies of the Liepāja Special Economic Zone conduct business activities and which is administered and supervised by the Liepāja Special Economic Zone Authority.

The territory of the Liepāja Special Economic Zone is defined in accordance with the territory plan and the description of the territorial boundaries of the Liepāja Special Economic Zone attached as an annex to the Law on the Liepāja Special Economic Zone.
