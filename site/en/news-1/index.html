As the development of the Liepaja Industrial Park on the site of the former “Liepājas metalurgs” steelworks continues, the Liepaja Special Economic Zone (Liepaja SEZ) Authority has announced an auction for the property at Eksporta Street 3, located within the Liepaja Industrial Park. Liepaja Industrial Park Development Continues with Site Remediation

29.09.2026.
Read more

The Liepaja Special Economic Zone Authority continues the transformation of the former steelworks “Liepajas metalurgs” industrial site by implementing the project “Development of the Liepaja Industrial Park Area – Phase 2.” The project involves redevelopment and remediation of a historically contaminated industrial site and construction of modern infrastructure required for business operations. The project is being implemented under the European Union Cohesion Policy Program 2021–2027, supporting the transition towards a climate-neutral economy and mitigating the socio-economic impacts associated with this transition in the Kurzeme region.

Decisions of the Liepaja SEZ Board of 25 September

28.09.2026.
Read more

The regular meeting of the Liepaja Special Economic Zone (Liepaja SEZ) Board was held on 25 September, during which decisions were adopted at the conclusion of an investment agreement, the non-exercise of pre-emption rights, and the organization of an auction, as well as amendments to previously adopted decisions. The Liepaja SEZ Board also approved the updated Development Plan of the Liepaja Special Economic Zone for 2018-2035 and the Development Program of the Port of Liepaja for 2023-2027.

Grain season gets off to a slower start at the Port of Liepaja this year

09.09.2026.
Read more

Stevedoring companies at the Port of Liepaja handled 341,830 tons of cargo in August 2026. Dry bulk cargo traditionally accounts for the largest share, with agricultural products, construction materials and wood chips totaling 188,160 tons. General cargo was the second-largest cargo group, accounting for 153,240 tons, of which 129,200 tons were transported on the regular Liepaja–Travemünde ferry service to Germany.
