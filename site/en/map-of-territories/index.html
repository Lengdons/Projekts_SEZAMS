Liepāja Industrial Park

The development of Liepāja Industrial Park is one of the most significant industrial development projects in Liepāja in recent years, implemented by the Liepāja Special Economic Zone Authority.

The aim of the project is to transform the territory of the former "Liepājas Metalurgs" factory into a modern and sustainable industrial park operating in the sectors defined by the Smart Specialisation Strategy (RIS3), promoting employment growth and creating high added value for the city of Liepāja and the Latvian economy as a whole.

Email: laura.adamovica@lsez.lv

Meldru Street Industrial Park

The Meldru Street development area is an alternative to the existing industrial areas in the city. The area is located in close proximity to the main Brīvības Street, providing companies operating in the area with convenient access to both the A9 highway and the Port of Liepāja without passing through the city's residential areas.

The area consists of three separate land plots equipped with the utilities required for development, with the support of Liepāja City Council and the European Regional Development Fund.

Port of Liepāja

Karosta Canal

The Karosta Canal is 3.5 kilometres long, up to 250 metres wide, and has an average depth of 9 metres. The canal was excavated between 1894 and 1901.

Oskars Kalpaks Bridge

The bridge opened on 19 August 1906 and is one of the oldest steel bridges in Latvia, as well as an engineering monument. It weighs 300 tonnes and consists of two sections (north and south), each formed by two identical cantilevers.
