Liepāja Industrial Park

The development of Liepāja Industrial Park is one of the most significant industrial development projects in Liepāja in recent years, implemented by the Liepāja Special Economic Zone Authority.

The project aims to transform the former Liepājas Metalurgs factory site into a modern, sustainable industrial park for sectors defined in the Smart Specialisation Strategy (RIS3), increasing employment and creating high added value for Liepāja and the Latvian economy.

Phone: (+371) 20501905

Email: laura.adamovica@lsez.lv

Meldru Street Industrial Park

The Meldru Street development area offers an alternative to the city’s existing industrial zones. Located immediately next to the major Brīvības Street, it provides businesses with convenient access to the A9 highway and the Port of Liepāja without passing through residential areas.

The area comprises three separate land plots equipped with the utilities required for development, with support from Liepāja City Council and the European Regional Development Fund.

Phone: (+371) 29294267
