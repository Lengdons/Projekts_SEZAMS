The O. Kalpaks Bridge will be closed to traffic for three days during an international film production

16.10.2026.
Read more

From 16 to 24 October, Liepaja will briefly become a filming location for an international production. The city will host filming of an adaptation of The Girl with the Dragon Tattoo, a novel in the internationally popular Millennium book series, produced by Left Bank Pictures, known for projects including The Crown and Dept Q. The Oskars Kalpaks Bridge has a special role in the production, having been selected as one of the series’ key filming locations. During three days of filming, the bridge will be closed to pedestrians and road vehicles, and shipping traffic will also be restricted.

Auction announced for property at 3 Eksporta Street in Liepaja Industrial Park

06.10.2026.
Read more

As development of Liepaja Industrial Park continues on the former Liepajas Metalurgs factory site, the Liepaja Special Economic Zone (Liepaja SEZ) Authority has announced an auction for real estate at 3 Eksporta Street, within Liepaja Industrial Park. This is the next step in involving businesses in the renewal and transformation of the industrial area, offering an opportunity to purchase production buildings together with the land.

Development of Liepaja Industrial Park continues with site remediation

29.09.2026.
Read more

The Liepaja SEZ Authority continues transforming the former Liepajas Metalurgs factory site through the project “Development of Liepaja Industrial Park, Phase 2”. The project involves improving and remediating a historically contaminated industrial area and building modern infrastructure needed by businesses. It is being implemented under the European Union Cohesion Policy Programme for 2021–2027, supporting the transition to a climate-neutral economy and reducing the socioeconomic impacts of that transition in the Kurzeme region.

Decisions of the Liepaja SEZ Board on 25 September

28.09.2026.
Read more

The regular meeting of the Liepaja SEZ Board took place on 25 September this year. Decisions included entering into an investment agreement, waiving pre-emption rights, holding an auction, and amending previously adopted decisions. The Board also approved the updated Liepaja Special Economic Zone Development Plan for 2018–2035 and the Liepaja Port Development Programme for 2023–2027.

Grain season starts later than usual at the Port of Liepaja this year

09.09.2026.
Read more

Stevedoring companies at the Port of Liepaja handled 341.83 thousand tonnes of various cargo in August 2026. As usual, the largest cargo group was bulk cargo — agricultural products, construction materials and wood chips — totalling 188.16 thousand tonnes. The second-largest group was general cargo at 153.24 thousand tonnes, of which 129.2 thousand tonnes was transported on the regular ferry route between Liepaja and Travemünde, Germany.
